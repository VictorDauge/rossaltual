About the company

Fashion with a personal fit.

Rossal Tual Immess Limited is a Papua New Guinean business specialising in clothing and footwear, with a strong focus on tailoring garments to the individual customer.

From professional suits and lawyer gowns to school uniforms, children's clothing, work wear, evening outfits and casual wear, customers can discuss their preferred style, fabric, colour, cut and finishing details.

Custom tailoring gives customers the opportunity to shape the final garment rather than simply choosing an off-the-rack size.

1
Share your idea
Tell us the occasion, style and look you want.

3

Your measurements are used to shape the garment for your preferred fit.
